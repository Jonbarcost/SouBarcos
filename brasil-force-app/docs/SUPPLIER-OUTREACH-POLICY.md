# Brasil Force — supplier acquisition and free promotion policy

**Status:** internal operating specification; not a binding contract or automated sending system.

## Supplier journey
1. Research lawful, publicly listed supplier contacts and record company, category, public source URL and contact method.
2. Human reviewer checks relevance, lawful products, business identity, data minimization and contact eligibility.
3. Send a personalized **draft only after human approval**, clearly identifying Brasil Force and the reason for contact. Do not scrape private addresses or bypass opt-outs.
4. Explain the **optional 30-day free catalog promotion**, its exact start date, what services are included and excluded, and the ability to cancel at any time.
5. Require express supplier permission before using product photos, brand names, prices, stock data or publishing a supplier catalog; record permission and its scope.
6. Start 30-day period only upon documented acceptance/activation. The marketplace is an independent platform, not an official military or police organization.
7. Send a renewal proposal sufficiently before expiry with **the actual maintenance fee and terms disclosed**; no automatic renewal or charging without a separate affirmative agreement.
8. On cancellation or expiry without paid agreement, unpublish listings and stop promotional use as agreed, while retaining only necessary compliance records.

## Operational guardrails
- No promise of guaranteed sales, traffic or rankings.
- No claim that paid maintenance is obligatory after the free period.
- No supplier impersonation, unauthorized brand use, misleading endorsements or restricted items.
- Honor unsubscribe requests and maintain a suppression list; do not re-contact opted-out suppliers.
- Keep auditable records of source, consent, content license, outreach and cancellation.
- Do not enable mass emailing or unattended outbound messaging until approval controls, quotas, domain reputation and compliance review exist.
