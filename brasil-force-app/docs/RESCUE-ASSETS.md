# Original hero artwork

`public/images/hero/serra-dawn.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool for Brasil Force on 2026-10-21 (São Paulo). No third-party stock photo was used. It is a fictional illustrative landscape, not evidence of an expedition, location visit or product available for sale. This is disclosed on the homepage.

Prompt: Original panoramic 16:9 cinematic Brazilian Serra do Mar / Atlantic rainforest landscape at dawn; gold sunlight through mist, deep forest green and graphite shadows, realistic editorial texture. Illuminated ridge on right; dark quiet negative space on left for text. No text, logos, people, products, weapons or buildings.

The generated 1672 × 941 image was converted to WebP quality 80 (172,908 bytes). Next Image serves responsive sizes with a reserved fill container and preload. Existing six original category SVGs and the PR #7 landscape SVG remain preserved. Inter and Manrope remain self-hosted through next/font; no new font families or runtime font services were added.
